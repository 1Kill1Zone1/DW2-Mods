Target: Steam beta1.3.8.7 package; see the bounded verification evidence below.

Scoped native qualification/frontier/save-load checks are recorded in the project history; live research UI remains manual.

Install with game/launcher closed, obtain the dependencies listed in dw2modlauncher.json separately, and use a disposable campaign/save slot.

1. On a disposable campaign, compare foreign-race colony population just below and at3,000,000,000 across owned colonies. Tourists and foreign-owned colonies must not count.
2. After qualification, inspect the research frontier: Additional Unlock Available should appear only for eligible unresearched technology with completed normal prerequisites and additional requirements.
3. Check an unaffordable eligible technology remains visible but cannot start without payment. Future tiers must not become selectable merely because the race qualified.
4. Save/reload after qualification and then reduce the population. The earned qualification remains; it does not grant free completed research or bypass ordinary prerequisites.
